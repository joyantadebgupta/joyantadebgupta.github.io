About Me

A brief introduction to my professional journey and passion.

I am an accomplished Electrical Design & Project Engineer with over 10 years of end-to-end execution — from 230/132kV grid substations like PGCB Aricha down to medium-voltage (33kV) utility systems, solar evacuation design up to 100 MW, industrial power infrastructure and compliance-driven factory electrification in Bangladesh.

I have a proven track record in leading 50+ high-stakes projects—from tendering and detailed design to erection, testing, and commissioning. My work ensures 100% adherence to BNBC, NFPA, NESCO, DESCO, and major buyer standards like RSC, Nirapon, Elevate, and Bureau Veritas. I also run insulation-resistance testing programmes and prepare utility bid packages with BOQs and vendor pricing.

Recognized for technical leadership and zero-incident safety performance, I have delivered mission-critical assets including 230kV grid bays, 33/11kV substations and 16–28 MVA power transformers, earning the trust of clients like PGCB, NESCO, DESCO, ACI, Epic Group, Siemens Bangladesh and the Bangladesh National Parliament.

Languages: Bengali (Native), English (Professional), Hindi (Conversational).
